Five gestures, each with a job

No shortcuts to memorize — one mouse action is enough

Click

Flip a full screen (80% by default). Rapid clicks accelerate — three clicks, three screens.

Click = flip a screen · faster when you chain
Press & hold

Scroll steadily, like dragging a scrollbar. Release or move away to stop immediately.

Hold = continuous scroll · stops on release
Right-click

Right-click the up button to jump to the very top; the down button reaches the very bottom.

Right-click = top / bottom
Ctrl + drag

Drag the buttons anywhere you like — the position is saved the moment you let go.

Anywhere · saved on release
Middle-click

Press the mouse wheel on either button to close the current tab instantly.

Middle-click = close tab · can be turned off
