Finding your reading rhythm,
one screen at a time

September 19, 2026 · about 12 minutes

This is a demo long-form page for screenshots. Scroll, pause, scroll again — reading should feel effortless, but the longer a page gets, the more your wrist complains.

Why it matters

Content platforms stack more into one page: comments, recommendations, promos. Sliding the wheel dozens of times to cross one screen adds up. A pair of tiny floating buttons turns all of that into a single click.

"The point of a tool is to make the repeated motion disappear." — a reader

Three everyday scenes

Long docs & codeFlip a full screen with one click — never lose your place.
Social feedsLet the button do the swiping; your thumb can rest.
Galleries & portfoliosHold for a continuous glide, like a smooth scrollbar.
Papers & ebooksRight-click to the very top or bottom — no Home-key mashing.

The little things

The buttons never get in the way: they fade out when idle, step aside in fullscreen videos, and you can drag them anywhere — or make them disappear on a specific site.

Keep scrolling — you'll spot the two little buttons on the right side of the page. Click, hold, right-click: each gesture has its own job.
