## Detailed description (ready to paste)

**Scrolling, one click away.**

"Scroll Buttons" floats two sleek little buttons on any web page, so you can scroll elegantly — one hand, one click, right where you want to be.

**▍Four gestures, each with a job**
· Click — smooth full-screen flip (80% by default); rapid clicks keep accelerating;
· Hold — continuous steady scrolling, stops the moment you release;
· Right-click — the up button jumps to the top of the page, the down button to the bottom;
· Ctrl + drag — place the buttons anywhere you like; position is saved on release.
A 1-minute animated tutorial opens automatically on first install — and stays available from the extension menu.

**▍36 styles in 6 families — looks matter**
Solid, Gradient, Glass, Breath (pulsing glow), Flow (drifting gradients) and Metal — six styles each, thirty-six in total. From quiet slate and charcoal to breathing neon lights, drifting aurora and nebula, brushed titanium and Klein Blue. Circle or rounded shapes; size and opacity fully adjustable.

**▍Quiet by design**
Fades away after a few idle seconds and comes back the instant your cursor approaches; hides itself in fullscreen; stays out of your way.

**▍Per-site exclusion**
Click the toolbar icon for a quick menu: disable/enable on the current site instantly (no refresh needed). The exclusion list is fully editable in settings — paste a full URL and the domain is recognized automatically (subdomains included).

**▍Backup & restore**
Settings live only in your local browser. Moving computers or reinstalling the browser? Export a backup file from the settings page and import it anytime — styles, position, speeds and the exclusion list, all restored in one click.

**▍Privacy promise**
No personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted. All settings live in your browser's local storage; the extension makes no network requests and contains no analytics or ads.
